  - What it changed (for later work): `scrollbar::ScrollWidth` (widest drawn line, only grows) on `View::scroll_width` and `DiffTab::scroll_width` (+ `DiffTab::text_width`, `keep_width`: edits to a diff's live buffer keep its width, other loads reset it); `Scroll::columns` + `Columns` (a `Scrollable` over `left_col`) behind `ScrollTarget::Columns(group)`; `app/scrollbar.rs`'s `along()` picks x or y so click / drag / hover serve both bars; `ui::scrollbar::take_row` / `render_row`; `GroupHit::hscrollbar`. The bar's row is decided from the last frame's sizes: a render that finds it wanted or gone sets `needs_redraw`, and `App::redraw` (used by `run`) draws up to 2 frames in one turn. `pos::col_after` measures a line's hidden rest (ASCII runs by bytes). Soft wrap must turn the bar and sideways scrolling off
  - Also: diff tabs widen a change's first row by its Stage / Unstage / Revert buttons, so a line under them scrolls fully into view; tests' `frame` / `one_frame` helpers (docs/dev/testing.md) check that rendering settles
  - Deferred: Shift+wheel on a hex tab still scrolls vertically (its rows fit, so nothing sideways)
  - Still to check by hand: a trackpad's sideways swipe / tilt wheel and Shift+wheel on long lines and diffs; no flicker when the bar appears (two frames in one turn); click, drag and hover colour on the bar; the blank corner cell; one bar across a side-by-side diff; a line under the Stage / Revert buttons fully readable at full scroll
  - Needs: the editor scroll bars (`scrollbar.rs`, `ui::scrollbar`, `ScrollTarget` / `ScrollBarHit`, `App::with_scrollable`), `View::left_col`, `DiffTab::left_col` / `scroll_columns`, `app/mouse.rs`'s wheel arm
  - The bug: a text tab's `left_col` only moves when the cursor is revealed. `mouse_event` handles `ScrollUp` / `ScrollDown` only, so a trackpad's sideways swipe or a tilt wheel (`ScrollLeft` / `ScrollRight`) does nothing, and Shift+wheel scrolls vertically (VS Code scrolls sideways). Diff tabs handle `ScrollLeft` / `ScrollRight` but `DiffTab::scroll_columns` has no upper bound, so they can scroll past every line into blank columns
  - The wheel: `ScrollLeft` / `ScrollRight`, and `ScrollUp` / `ScrollDown` with Shift, scroll the text or diff tab under the pointer sideways by a step (the diff view's `SIDE_STEP`), without focusing it or moving the cursor (as the vertical wheel does); the same over its scroll bars. The next cursor reveal scrolls back as it does now. No sideways scroll while soft wrap is on (once the soft wrap task lands; see [Editor](../PLAN.md#editor)) or on a hex tab (its rows always fit)
  - The scroll width: the widest line drawn in that view so far (display columns, measured as visible lines are drawn: never a scan of the whole buffer, since a 1 MB file must stay in the frame budget), at least `left_col + width`. It only grows, as VS Code's does, so the bar doesn't come and go as you scroll vertically; it is reset when the view is reloaded or its buffer replaced. Scrolling right stops when the widest line's end is in view (one blank column after it, like the vertical bar's last page)
  - The bar: a one-row bar on the editor's last row, left of the vertical bar's column, shown only while the scroll width is wider than the view (VS Code's `auto`). The row is taken from the text area only then, so text and diff tabs whose lines all fit lose no row. Thumb, track, hover and drag look and act as the vertical bar does, with the same `scrollbarSlider.*` theme keys. A click on the track moves the thumb there and a drag scrolls. The corner cell where the two bars meet is blank. Side-by-side diffs get one bar spanning both sides, since their `left_col` is shared. `view.width` / `height`, the find widget, popups and `GroupHit::editor` use the area that is left
  - Sessions and back / forward already keep `left_col`; the scroll width isn't saved
  - Done when: in a text tab with long lines, a sideways wheel event and Shift+wheel scroll it sideways without moving the cursor, stopping at the widest line's end; the bar appears only once a line wider than the view has been drawn, its thumb follows the scroll, and a click or drag on it scrolls; the same in side-by-side and inline diff tabs; a diff tab no longer scrolls past its lines; typing in a 1 MB file stays within the frame budget
