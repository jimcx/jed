  - What it changed (for later work): `app/tab_drag.rs`: `Drag::Tab(TabDrag { from, index, tab, target })` — `tab` is the dragged tab's identity at the press (`TabIdentity`, see `group.rs`: a text tab's buffer, a diff or hex tab's unique id, or a shell's run), and a release whose tab at `index` is no longer the same one is cancelled (a tab closed, opened or reordered with a key mid-drag); `TabDrop::TabBar { group, index }` (a gap among the group's tabs, an absolute index) and `TabDrop::Editor { group, side: Option<DropSide> }` (`None` = the centre); `snap_side(rect, x, y)` (the nearest edge within a quarter of that dimension, inclusive, ties Left, Right, Up, Down; a dimension that can't hold two minimum groups is no side and the drop is a centre move), `App::tab_drag_to` / `finish_tab_drag` / `tab_drop_in` / `tab_drop_shade`. `group.rs`: `Group::move_tab(from, to)` (the final index), `Group::insert(tab, at)` (`take` delegates to it), `Groups::move_tab_between(from, index, to, at)` (`Some` gap, `None` = right of the target's active tab), `Groups::split_empty_of(target, side)`. `layout.rs`: `DropSide` next to `SplitDir`, `SplitTree::split_with(target, dir, new, new_first)` (`split` delegates with `false`). Drawing: `ui::tabs`'s `tab.dropMarker` cell at a bar gap; `ui::mod` shades `editor.dropBackground` after the groups and before the dividers — the half a side would split off, or the whole editor for the centre
  - Also: a shell tab moves with the rest (a move, never a copy, so its run and output continue); a release seals undo, then focuses and reveals the tab in its new group; a source group left with no tabs closes
  - Deferred: a thumbnail or any preview in the shade (it's a flat colour); a separate "can't drop here" look for a pane too small to split (it falls back to a centre move); an editor's scroll bar column and horizontal bar row are a one-cell dead zone (the hit uses `GroupHit::editor`); the shaded half is the editor rect's half, so it can be a column / row off the eventual divider; an own-group centre drop shades the editor but moves nothing (as designed)
  - Still to check by hand: each of the four edge bands in a real terminal (the shade readable over text, the new split's placement, also with the side panel narrow); a centre drop; a lone tab's own-edge drop (the band shades, then the window returns to one group); Esc and a release over a divider / the side panel / the status bar; a bar drag showing the marker and no shade; a pane too narrow or short to split falling back to a centre move; dragging a shell tab to another group (its output carries on); Cmd+W mid-drag leaving the neighbour where it was
  - Needs: Move tabs ✅ (moving a tab keeps its view; a group left empty closes)
  - Snapping: a drop within a quarter of an editor's width or height of one of its edges splits that group on that side and the tab lands in the new split; the rest of the editor moves the tab into that group, activated
  - Done when: dragging a tab along its bar reorders it, its drop marker following the pointer; dropping it on another group's tab bar moves it there at the marker (the source group closes when that was its last tab) with its selections and scroll kept; dropping it on an editor's centre moves it into that group, activated; dropping it within the snap band of an editor's left / right / top / bottom edge splits that group on that side and the tab lands in the new split, the band shaded while dragging; a drag that never leaves its tab is just a click; Esc or a release outside any target leaves every group as it was
