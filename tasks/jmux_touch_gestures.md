See [jmux's plan](../jmux/PLAN.md): [Terminals, tabs and splits](../jmux/PLAN.md#terminals-tabs-and-splits), and [jmux_touch](jmux_touch.md).

In the touch layout (`--touch`) a press on a tab starts a drag at once, as with a mouse. With a finger that is the wrong default: the natural move on a tab bar that overflows is to swipe it sideways, and today that swipe drags the tab you happened to land on into another group or a split. A phone's answer is the one to copy: a swipe scrolls, a tap selects, and a hold (finger still for about half a second) picks the item up, after which it follows the finger until it's put down. This applies only to a client attached with `--touch`; a normal client keeps today's press-and-drag.

  - Needs: `jmux_touch` (`App::touch`, `jtui::tabs::TabMetrics`, the 3-row bar and its hit map); `jmux/src/app.rs` (`pointer_event`, `Drag`, `AppEvent`, the event sender used for `Tick`), `jmux/src/tab_drag.rs` (`TabDrag`, `tab_drag_to`, `finish_tab_drag`), `jmux/src/group.rs` (`scroll_tabs`), `jmux/src/hit.rs` (`Hit::Tab` / `Close` / `Arrow` / `NewTab` / `Bar`, `TabHit` x ranges), `jmux/src/tabbar.rs` (drawing the lifted tab), `jmux/src/theme.rs`, `crates/jtui` (a new `touch` module, shared with `jed_touch`).
  - The gesture, in `jtui::touch` so `jed_touch` uses the same rules: a pure state machine with no clock of its own (`Press::new(at)`, `moved(at) -> Gesture`, `held() -> Gesture`, `released(at) -> Gesture`) and the constants `HOLD` (450 ms) and `SLOP` (1 cell: motion within it on either axis is still a hold or a tap, so finger jitter doesn't turn a tap into a swipe). The outcomes: `Tap` (released within `SLOP` before `HOLD`), `Swipe { dx }` (moved past `SLOP` before `HOLD`; reports the column delta since the last motion), `Lifted` (`HOLD` passed within `SLOP`), then `Drag` motion and `Drop` as today. Unit tests at the bottom of the module cover each transition.
  - The hold timer: a left press on the bar in the touch layout records a pending press (`Drag::Press { group, hit, gesture, serial }`) and spawns a one-shot `tokio::time::sleep(HOLD)` that sends `AppEvent::Hold(serial)`. A stale serial (the press already became a tap or a swipe, or a newer press started) is ignored. Without the timer a finger held still sends no events, so the pick-up could not be shown until the finger moved.
  - Swipe: motion past `SLOP` on any press that started on the bar (a tab, its `×`, an arrow, the `+` or the empty part) scrolls that bar. The tabs follow the finger: moving left brings later tabs in. `scroll_tabs` moves by whole tabs, so the motion accumulates and scrolls one tab each time it passes the width of the tab entering or leaving the left edge (`TabMetrics::tab_width` of its title), carrying the remainder, so the bar keeps roughly under the finger. Only `dx` counts; the finger leaving the bar vertically keeps scrolling until release. A swipe never focuses or closes anything.
  - Tap, acted on at release instead of press: a tab focuses it (and its group), `×` closes (the running-command question as today), an arrow scrolls one tab, `+` opens a tab in that group, the empty bar focuses the group. A tap therefore needs the release to land within `SLOP` of the press. Wheel events on the bar keep scrolling as today (some SSH apps turn a swipe into the wheel).
  - Hold: on `AppEvent::Hold` for a press on a tab (not `×`, an arrow or `+`; a hold on those does nothing and the release is a tap), that tab is lifted: it becomes the active tab of its group, `drag` becomes `Drag::Tab` as a mouse press makes it today, and the frame draws it with a new theme key `tab.lifted` (in `own_entries`) so the finger has visible feedback before it moves. From then on motion and release go through `tab_drag_to` / `finish_tab_drag` unchanged: reorder, another bar, another group's terminal, or a split edge. Released without moving, it stays where it was.
  - A normal (non-touch) client: unchanged, press-and-drag at once and actions on press. The branch is one check of `App::touch` at the start of the bar's left-press handling.
  - Not in this task: gestures in the terminal area (swipe to scroll back, hold to select), dividers, the side bar; see Deferred.
  - Done when: with `--touch`, a swipe starting on a tab, on `×`, on an arrow or on the empty bar scrolls an overflowing bar in the finger's direction and neither moves, focuses nor closes anything; a tap on a tab focuses it, on `×` closes it (asking when a command runs), on an arrow scrolls, on `+` opens a tab; a press held still for `HOLD` lifts the tab (drawn with `tab.lifted`) and then drags it to a gap in its own bar, another bar, another group's terminal and a split edge; jitter within `SLOP` still taps or holds; a stale hold timer does nothing; a plain client's press still drags at once; `scripts/check` is green. Tests in `jmux/src/jmux_touch_gestures_tests.rs` (`//!` doc, `#[cfg(test)] mod` line in `main.rs`), named `jmux_touch_gestures_*`: they deliver `AppEvent::Hold(serial)` directly rather than sleeping, plus one test that the real timer lifts a tab (settled on the lifted tab, not on time). `jmux_touch_a_tab_drags_to_another_bar_and_to_a_terminal_half` in `jmux_touch_tests.rs` moves to the hold-then-drag form (its press-and-move becomes a swipe).
  - Docs: `jmux/README.md` (the `--touch` row: swipe, tap, hold to move), `jmux/PLAN.md` (Terminals, tabs and splits: the touch gestures), [crates](../docs/dev/crates.md) (`jtui::touch`), and `jed_touch`'s "Needs" gains `jtui::touch` so jed's bars behave the same.
  - Deferred: a hold then release without moving opening a tab menu (close, close others, split right / down, move to group n), for a screen with no middle or right click; the same gestures over a terminal (swipe scrolls the scrollback when the program hasn't asked for the mouse, hold starts a selection); `HOLD` / `SLOP` as settings.
  - Still to check by hand: in a tablet's and a phone's SSH app (e.g. Termius, Blink), whether a long press reaches jmux as a still press or the app takes it for its own selection / paste menu (if the app takes it, tabs can't be picked up there and move-tab commands are the fallback); whether a swipe arrives as mouse drags or as wheel events; whether 450 ms feels right.
- ✅ Done. With `--touch`, a swipe on a tab bar scrolls its tabs under the finger, a tap acts on release (focus, close with the running-command question, scroll, new tab, focus the group), and a press held still for `HOLD` lifts a tab (drawn `tab.lifted`) for an ordinary tab drag; a plain client keeps press-and-drag.
  - `jtui::touch` (`Press`, `Gesture { Idle, Tap, Swipe { dx }, Lifted, Drag, Drop }`, `HOLD`, `SLOP`) is the clock-free state machine `jed_touch` reuses. jmux drives it from `jmux/src/app/touch.rs`: `Drag::Press { group, hit, gesture, carry, serial }`, `AppEvent::Hold(serial)` from a one-shot `sleep(HOLD)`, `press_moved` (swipe with a carried remainder), `press_released`, `hold`.
  - `tabbar::max_scroll(widths, width, metrics)` is shared by the renderer and the swipe, so a fast swipe stops at the scroll the bar actually draws; `tabbar::render` takes `Marks { drop, lifted }`.
  - Also: `jmux_touch_tests.rs`'s `Harness` is `pub(crate)` and reused by `jmux_touch_gestures_tests.rs`; its `press_and_move` now presses, delivers `Hold`, then drags.
  - Deferred: as listed above (a tab menu on hold-and-release, gestures over a terminal, `HOLD` / `SLOP` as settings).
  - Still to check by hand: as listed above (whether a long press reaches jmux as a still press in Termius / Blink, whether a swipe arrives as drags or wheel events, whether 450 ms feels right).
  - Superseded in part by [jmux_touch_menu](jmux_touch_menu.md): the touch layout has no tab bars any more, so the bar's swipe, tap and hold-to-lift are gone. The terminal gestures remain.
